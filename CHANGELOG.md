# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- AirPlay receiver: pick "BeamNG" in the iPhone's AirPlay menu and the music plays in the car.
- Track title, artist, artwork and progress on a compact head unit screen.
- Play/pause, previous and next from the car control the phone.
- One volume, the phone's: the mouse wheel or `−`/`+` on the screen move the slider on the iPhone,
  and the phone's slider moves the number on the screen. A new connection starts at the volume
  you listened at last.
- Sounds like a car stereo: clear in the cabin, muffled from outside the car, quiet when walking.
- Settings behind a gear on the screen: "Full sound outside the car" for playing in third person,
  and the overall volume of the mod.
- The screen appears while you sit in a car, can be dragged anywhere and minimized.
- English and Russian, following the game language.
